Cách dùng TMUX
  1. Connect via SSH to your VM and navigate to the project directory:
    cd /path/to/FedMedAI_Study
    chmod +x test.sh

  2. Start a persistent session using tmux:
    tmux new -s fed 
    chạy test.sh ví dụ:
    ./test.sh "fedprox" baseline_bn "bloodmnist organamnist pathmnist"
    # Hoặc chạy chiến lược CLA (Coverage + Logit Adjustment):
    ./test.sh "cla" baseline_bn "bloodmnist organamnist pathmnist"

  3. Detach and disconnect safely:
      • Press Ctrl + B, then release and press D.
      • You can now safely close your SSH terminal or shut down your laptop; the experiments will keep running on the
      VM GPU.
  4. Reattach anytime to view live progress:
    tmux attach -t fed
