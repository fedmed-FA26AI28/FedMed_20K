 %Section - 1.1 
Medical image classification is a fundamental task in computer-aided diagnosis, where deep learning models automatically assign disease or tissue categories to clinical images. Deploying accurate classification systems across hospitals and clinical centers has the potential to reduce diagnostic burden on clinicians, accelerate screening workflows, and improve early intervention rates. However, training such models typically requires large-scale, diverse imaging datasets aggregated from multiple institutions — a process that directly conflicts with patient privacy regulations such as Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and  General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Federated Learning (FL) offers a principled solution to this dilemma by enabling multiple clients — each representing a hospital, imaging lab, or edge device — to collaboratively train a shared global model while keeping all patient data local, communicating only model parameter updates to a central server.
